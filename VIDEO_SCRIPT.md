### 🎬 SCENE 3 — CITIZEN WORKFLOW DEMO (0:45 – 1:20)

**[VISUAL]:** Live screen recording of the citizen flow:
1. Click "Register" → Fill form → Account created
2. Click "Report Issue" → Select category (e.g., "Sewage Mixing"), type description, upload photo, select area, mark location → Submit
3. See the complaint appear in "My Complaints" with auto-assigned priority badge (Critical 🔴)
4. Click into complaint → Show the timeline tracker

**[VOICEOVER]:**

> *"Let me show you how it works. A citizen registers in seconds and reports a water issue — they select the problem type, describe it, upload a photo, and mark the location.*
>
> *The moment they submit, our Smart Priority Engine kicks in. It calculates a severity score based on the issue type, whether it's a drinking water problem, whether evidence was attached, and how many other complaints already exist in that area.*
>
> *In this case — sewage mixing, with a photo, in an area with 5 existing complaints — the system automatically flags it as Critical.*
>
> *The citizen can track their complaint status in real-time, just like tracking a delivery — from Submitted, to Under Review, to Assigned, to In Progress, to Resolved."*

**[TEXT ON SCREEN]:**
```
Sewage Mixing (50) + Drinking Water (+15) + Image (+10) + Area Load (+20)
= Score 95 → 🔴 CRITICAL
```

>
> *When an area crosses the risk threshold, JalRakshak automatically generates community alerts — warning citizens to boil water, avoid drinking directly, or take precautions.*

> *Each area also gets a dynamic water safety score that updates based on complaint activity."*

## 🎙️ VOICEOVER TIPS

1. **Pace:** Speak at a steady, confident pace — not too fast, not too slow
2. **Emphasis:** Stress keywords like "smart", "automatically", "real-time", "transparent"
3. **Pauses:** Brief pauses after key stats (780 million, 70%) for impact
4. **Tone:** Professional but passionate — you care about this problem
5. **Energy:** Build energy gradually — start calm, end strong

## 📹 RECORDING TIPS

1. **Screen Recording:** Use OBS Studio or Loom for smooth 1080p capture
2. **Browser:** Use Chrome in full screen, hide bookmarks bar
3. **Tab:** Only JalRakshak tab open — no distractions
4. **Mouse Movement:** Slow, deliberate cursor movements
5. **Transitions:** Use simple fade or slide transitions between scenes
6. **Music:** Use royalty-free ambient music from YouTube Audio Library
7. **Thumbnail:** Create a custom thumbnail with the JalRakshak logo and "Demo Video" text
