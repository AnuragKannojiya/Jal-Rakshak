Main innovation points

These are the features that can help you win:

1. Smart priority engine

Complaint gets auto-priority:

Low
Medium
High
Critical

Based on:

issue type
repeated complaints from same area
whether image is attached
whether complaint is drinking-water related
number of unresolved complaints nearby

This can be rule-based and still look smart.

Example:

“Dirty drinking water” + 5 reports in same area = Critical
“Leakage in lane” = Medium
“No water supply for full locality” = High

This adds intelligence without needing heavy AI.

2. Community water safety alerts

When an area has many unresolved complaints, show:

Unsafe Area
Boil water before use
Use filtered water
Avoid drinking directly
Report symptoms to health center if contamination suspected

This creates public value.

3. Heatmap / issue map

Use map markers for complaints:

red = critical
orange = high
yellow = medium
green = resolved

This is very visual and judges will like it immediately.

4. Live analytics dashboard

Show:

total complaints
resolved complaints
most affected areas
issue category breakdown
trend graph
response time
complaint-to-resolution ratio

This makes the project feel official.

5. Area-wise water score

Each area gets a water safety score:

90–100 = Safe
70–89 = Moderate
40–69 = Risky
0–39 = Unsafe

Score can depend on:

complaint volume
unresolved issue count
complaint type severity
recent quality update

This becomes your unique feature.

What judges will ask, and your best answers
1. Why SDG 6?

Because water quality and sanitation directly affect health, daily life, and community well-being. JalRakshak creates a practical digital system for faster reporting, visibility, and action.

2. What is innovative here?

Our platform combines complaint management, area-wise safety scoring, smart priority generation, community alerts, and analytics in one system.

3. Who will use it?

Citizens, students, housing societies, local authorities, municipal teams, and community volunteers.

4. Is it scalable?

Yes. It can expand from college/locality level to municipal ward level with area-based management and dashboard reporting.

5. What makes it better than a normal complaint form?

It is not just a submission form. It has tracking, prioritization, alerts, analytics, and transparency.
